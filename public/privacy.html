Privacy notice

Kairo is a career tool. This page says what happens to your data.

Your data stays in your browser

Your profile, CV, saved roles and stories are kept in this browser's local storage (keys starting with co_). Kairo has no accounts, no database, no cookies and no analytics or trackers.

When you run a tool

The text needed for that tool (for example the job description, and your CV or profile where the tool uses it) is sent through our server to Anthropic's AI to produce the result. We don't store it: our server does not log or save the content of requests.

Who processes data

  • Vercel Inc. – hosting of the website and the server function. Technical request data such as your IP address is processed to deliver the site.
  • Anthropic PBC – the AI model that produces results. Data is transferred to the United States.

Fonts and the PDF reader are served from this site itself. Your browser makes no requests to other third parties when the page loads.

Deleting your data

Use the backup/export option if you want a copy. To delete everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings (or remove the co_ entries in local storage). Nothing remains on our side.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no copy of your data, deletion is done by you in your browser as described above.
