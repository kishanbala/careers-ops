Grade Guide

A 4.5–5.0
Strongly Recommend — Exceptional fit. Apply immediately.
A- 4.0–4.4
Recommend — Strong fit with minor gaps. Worth applying.
B+ 3.5–3.9
Consider with Caution — Decent overlap but meaningful gaps.
B 3.0–3.4
Neutral — Partial fit. Only proceed with a strong referral.
C 2.5–2.9
Not Recommended — Weak fit. More gaps than strengths.
D < 2.5
Skip — Poor fit. Applying wastes everyone's time.

Philosophy: only apply to 4.0+ scores. Below that you spend interviews defending gaps rather than selling strengths.
